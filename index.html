4. 日常聊天话题库（25个）

避开政治、宗教、枪支、中美政治比较等话题；有人带节奏就说 “Let's keep it fun — next topic!” 换题。

1. 美食 · 早餐对决
Real talk: what did you eat for breakfast? I had dumplings this morning. No regrets.
追问：Is cereal breakfast or dessert? / Sweet or savory breakfast people?
说真的你早餐吃啥？我今早吃的饺子，不后悔。追问：麦片算早餐还是甜品？甜党还是咸党？
2. 美食 · 快餐排名
Rank fast food for me. Go. I'll start: Chick-fil-A is top three.
追问：Why is it closed on Sundays?! / What's your go-to order?
给快餐排个名，我先来：Chick-fil-A 前三。追问：为啥周日不开门？你必点啥？
3. 美食 · 美式中餐
Confession: I never had orange chicken until I learned about America. Is it good?
追问：What's your Panda Express order? / Want to see what a real Chinese breakfast looks like?
坦白：我之前没吃过陈皮鸡，好吃吗？追问：熊猫快餐你点啥？想知道真正的中国早餐长啥样吗？
4. 美食 · 奇怪吃法
Weirdest food combo you secretly love? Mine is fries dipped in ice cream.
追问：Who taught you that? / Would you try mine?
你偷偷爱吃的奇怪组合？我的是薯条蘸冰淇淋。追问：谁教你的？你敢试我的吗？
5. 美食 · 夜宵
It's getting late — what's your midnight snack? Be honest.
追问：Instant noodles people, type 1. That's literally our gang name.
有点晚了，你夜宵吃啥？说实话。追问：泡面党扣1，这就是我们团名。（深夜段用）
6. 生活 · 猫派狗派
Dog person or cat person? Type D or C.
追问：What's your pet's name? / What's the dumbest thing your pet ever did?
狗派还是猫派？打D或C。追问：宠物叫啥？它干过最蠢的事？
7. 生活 · 今天的小好事
Tell me one good thing that happened today. Tiny counts.
追问：Okay, that's adorable. / Anyone need a hype-up instead?
说一件今天的好事，小事也算。追问：好可爱。有人需要我给你打打气吗？
8. 生活 · 下班放学吐槽
Who just got off work or school? How bad was it, 1 to 10?
追问：What would make it a 10? / Who's your work enemy? No names.
谁刚下班放学？今天糟糕程度1到10？追问：怎样能到10分？你的职场死对头是谁？别说名字。（开播 6–7 PM PT 用，美西刚下班）
9. 生活 · 周末计划
What are you doing this weekend? Me? This. Streaming. Living my dream.
追问：Any concerts or games? / Who's doing absolutely nothing? Respect.
周末干嘛？我？就这，直播，活出梦想。追问：有演唱会或球赛吗？谁打算啥也不干？尊重。
10. 生活 · 时差
Fun fact: it's morning for me right now. So technically, good morning AND good night.
追问：Night owls or early birds, which one are you?
冷知识：我这边现在是早上，所以早安和晚安一起说。追问：你是夜猫子还是早起党？（北京时间上午，即开播头两小时 6–8 PM PT 用；按主播是否愿意透露所在地使用）
11. 中美文化 · 小费
Can someone explain tipping to me? Why does the iPad at the coffee shop ask me for 25%?
追问：Do you tip for coffee? / In China we don't really tip. Wild, right?
谁给我讲讲小费？为啥咖啡店的平板问我要25%？追问：买咖啡给小费吗？中国基本不给小费，离谱吧？
12. 中美文化 · 冰水 vs 热水
Why does America put ice in everything? I drink hot water. Fight me.
追问：Hot water gang, type 1. Ice gang, type 2.
美国为啥啥都加冰？我喝热水，不服来辩。追问：热水党扣1，冰水党扣2。（Fight me 为可选俚语）
13. 中美文化 · 节日（时令）
Thanksgiving is this week — what's the best dish? Wrong answers only.
追问：Is turkey actually good, or just tradition? / Are you doing Black Friday?
这周感恩节，最好吃的菜是啥？只许说错误答案。追问：火鸡真好吃还是只是传统？你冲黑五吗？（节日换成当周节日）
14. 中美文化 · 美国口头禅
Teach me one word Americans say all the time that I'll never get.
追问：Use it in a sentence. / Okay, I'm gonna say it wrong. Ready?

15. 中美文化 · 中文小课堂
Want to learn one Chinese word? Today's word: jiāyóu. It means 'you got this.'
追问：Type it in the chat! / Now yell it at me when I dance.
想学个中文词吗？今天的词：加油，意思是“你可以的”。追问：打在公屏！我跳舞时冲我喊。
16. 中美文化 · 刻板印象真假
What's one thing you think everyone in China does? I'll tell you if it's true.
追问：True or false — let's play. / Now tell me one American thing I should know.
你觉得中国人都会做的一件事？我告诉你真假。追问：来玩真假题。换你告诉我一个美国冷知识。

Day [X] of learning to dance. Be honest — am I improving, or just more confident?
